Alonovo products: data model and API

Draft 2026-10-26. now = in the code today, proposed = to add, outside = another system.

Who uses it

  • A shopper searches "cheerios" and sees the product, its maker's grade, and a closer better option (Life, Purely O's).
  • A shopper scans a barcode in a store and gets the same answer.
  • A front end anywhere (shop page, mobile app, someone else's site) gets all of this from the API.

The chain

USDA FoodData Central outside

455,458 US packaged foods, file of 2026-04-30. Barcode, description, brand, brand owner, category, ingredients, nutrition.

Public domain. New file about every six months.

  • data/brand_owner_reviewed.jsonl: 56 names checked by hand

BrandMapping now

  • brand_name → company, role

138 rows. Used by barcode scan to guess the company. No change.

The rules

  • Every product has a graded company. Products whose owner we cannot map stay in the USDA file, not the table.
  • Nothing from a source is dropped: the whole record sits in attributes.
  • The 345 hand-entered products merge onto their USDA row when the barcode matches; the rest stay as they are.
  • Every import is a command in the repo, saves as it goes, and can be rerun on the next USDA file.

Questions

  1. Aisles. USDA has its own categories ("Cereal", "Chips, Pretzels & Snacks"…). (a) map them onto our 17 aisles, or (b) use USDA's categories as the aisles?
  2. Names. USDA descriptions are often upper case ("CAP'N CRUNCH CEREAL"). Keep as given, or show in title case?
  3. Better option. Same USDA category, a better grade, then closest sugar and first ingredient. Anything else that makes two products "the same thing"?
  4. Makers we don't grade (Three Wishes, local granola): leave out for now, or load with no grade?
